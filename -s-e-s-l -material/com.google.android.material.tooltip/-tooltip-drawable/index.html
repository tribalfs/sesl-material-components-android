SESL Material/com.google.android.material.tooltip/TooltipDrawable

TooltipDrawable

@RestrictTo(value = LIBRARY_GROUP)
open class TooltipDrawable : MaterialShapeDrawable, TextDrawableHelper.TextDrawableDelegate

A Tooltip that supports shape theming and draws a pointer on the bottom in the center of the supplied bounds. Additional margin can be applied which will prevent the main bubble of the Tooltip from being drawn too close to the edge of the window.

Note: setRelativeToView should be called so TooltipDrawable can calculate where it is being drawn within the visible display.
